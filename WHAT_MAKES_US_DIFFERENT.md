# What Makes Us Different

• **Solved the OMR Problem with Browser Automation**: Optical Music Recognition (OMR) is notoriously difficult with no reliable APIs available. We innovated by using Playwright to automate SoundSlice's web interface, effectively turning their human-facing OMR service into an automated pipeline that extracts structured MusicXML from sheet music images.

• **Complete End-to-End Hardware Solution**: Unlike software-only music apps, we built a full-stack system with custom firmware on the T5AI development board that records audio, displays real-time feedback on an embedded screen, and provides TTS coaching—all while syncing with mobile and cloud services.

• **Real-Time Performance Analysis with AI Coaching**: Our system doesn't just detect wrong notes—it uses advanced pitch tracking (PYIN algorithm), timing analysis, and Gemini AI to generate personalized, actionable coaching feedback that helps musicians improve specific techniques and understand their mistakes in context.

• **Standalone Practice with Embedded Display**: Unlike apps that require a phone or tablet, our T5AI device can operate independently with its embedded screen showing real-time note detection, fingering charts, and visual feedback—enabling focused practice sessions without distractions from notifications or other apps.
